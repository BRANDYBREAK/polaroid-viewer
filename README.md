# 📸 Aesthetic Polaroid Gallery (No-Code Config)

A mobile-friendly, swipeable 3D Polaroid photo viewer built with React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, and Cloudflare Pages.

## 🚀 How to Make Your Own in 2 Minutes

### 1. Fork this Template
Click the green **"Use this template"** button above to create your own copy on GitHub.

### 2. Add Your Photos & Customize
* **Photos:** Drop your photos into `src/assets/photos/`.
* **Settings:** Edit `public/config.txt` right on GitHub to change the title, curator name, and background:

\`\`\`ini
galleryTitle = For Kathleen
author = Your Name
source = auto
background = #e8e3d9
textColor = #292524
\`\`\`

### 3. Deploy Free to Cloudflare Pages
1. Log into [Cloudflare Dashboard](https://dash.cloudflare.com/) > **Workers & Pages**.
2. Click **Create application** > **Pages** > **Connect to Git**.
3. Select your repository, pick the **Vite** preset, and click **Save and Deploy**.

Your gallery will be live instantly on a free `*.pages.dev` domain!
